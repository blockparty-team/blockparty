About

3 days of electornic wonders in the woods. Experience a joyful journey in our enchanted forest with groundbreaking artists, otherworldly performances and joyrides.

This year’s headliners include Amelie Lens, horsegiirL, Monolink, Mischluft, Christian Löffler, Ellen Allien, Bad Boombox, and NOTION.

Karrusel takes place at a secluded green spot on Refshaleøen in central Copenhagen.
